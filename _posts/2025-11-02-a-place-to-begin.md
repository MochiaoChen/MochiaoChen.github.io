This is the first entry on this website, and a small commitment: I want what lives here to be more than a stream of immediate opinions. It should be work that has been organised, can be revisited, and remains open to correction.

## What I plan to write

Most entries will fall into three broad groups:

1. **Research notes** on AI for Finance, computational economics, NLP, and market simulation;
2. **Project retrospectives** on why a tool was built, what worked, and what did not;
3. **Essays** on learning, writing, and questions that deserve a second look.

## Why an independent journal

Social media is good at moving information. A journal is better at giving it structure. An article can have a table of contents, footnotes, quotations, figures, and code—and it can still be revised years later without answering to a timeline.

> A useful note does not preserve everything. It leaves a reliable place from which to think again.
